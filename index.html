SBIRT Prenatal Screening

UA College of Medicine – Phoenix · For clinical provider use

Universal pre-screen

Two quick questions for all pregnant patients at every prenatal visit.

Alcohol
In the past year, have you had any drinks containing alcohol?
Substances
In the past year, have you used tobacco, marijuana, or any other drugs?
This screening tool uses the validated T-ACE and 4P's Plus protocols. For use by licensed clinical providers only. Results do not replace clinical judgment.

T-ACE alcohol screening

Validated for alcohol use in pregnancy (Sokol et al., 1989). Score ≥2 indicates risk.

T — Tolerance
How many drinks does it take to make you feel high?
A — Annoyed
Have people annoyed you by criticizing your drinking?
C — Cut down
Have you ever felt you should cut down on your drinking?
E — Eye-opener
Have you ever had a drink first thing in the morning to steady your nerves or get rid of a hangover?

Substance use screening

4P's Plus — validated for use in prenatal settings (Chasnoff et al.).

Past use
Before you knew you were pregnant, how often did you use alcohol?
Marijuana
Have you used marijuana in the past month?
Prescription misuse
Have you used prescription medications not prescribed to you, or differently than prescribed?
Other substances
Have you used any other drugs in the past year? (cocaine, heroin, methamphetamine, etc.)
Partner use
Does your partner currently use alcohol or drugs?

Screening results

Provider review only — do not display directly to patient.
